📊 ANALYSIS
Float Length Checker
Highlights warp and weft floats exceeding your maximum. Floats longer than 4–5 threads can snag and weaken fabric.
Thread Count — EPI / PPI
Selvedge Preview
Shows the first and last N warp ends to preview how the edges will look. A good selvedge has consistent interlacement at the edges.
Structure Identifier
Analyses the threading and tie-up to identify the weave structure.
Threading Repeat Finder
Detects the smallest repeating unit in the threading sequence.
Tie-Up Complexity
Checks how many shafts must be lifted simultaneously and flags any combinations that may exceed your loom's capacity.

📤 EXPORT & SHARE
Drawdown — PNG
Export the drawdown grid as a PNG image at 10px per cell.
Full Draft — PDF
Complete weaving draft with threading, tie-up, treadling, drawdown, and colour strips. Opens print dialog — save as PDF.
Seamless Tile — PNG
Exports the drawdown as a tileable PNG for use in Photoshop, Illustrator, or fabric mockup tools. The edges are designed to repeat seamlessly.
Threading Instructions — Text
Human-readable threading, treadling, and tie-up notation. Print and pin to your loom.
Dobby Peg Plan — Printable
Converts your tie-up and treadling into a dobby peg plan — one row per pick showing which shafts to raise. Also renders a Jacquard-style binary layout.
Draft Notation Export
Export the draft in formats compatible with common weaving software, or as a plain text drawdown matrix.
🔀 PATTERN TOOLS
Flip & Rotate — Drawdown
Transforms the entire drawdown pattern. Threading, tie-up and treadling are unchanged.
Invert Drawdown
Swaps every warp-up cell to weft-down and vice versa — reverses the entire interlacement. Like switching from face to back.
Repeat / Tile Selection
Use the □ Select tool to drag a region on the drawdown, then tile it across the whole grid. Or enter a manual block size below.
